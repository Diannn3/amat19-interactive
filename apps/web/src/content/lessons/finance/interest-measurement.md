## The model question
Before using a formula, decide what earns interest. In **simple interest**, only the original principal earns interest. In **compound interest**, the accumulated balance becomes the base for the next period.

## Two accumulation models
For principal `P`, annual rate `i`, and time `t`:

- Simple: `A = P(1 + it)`
- Effective compound: `A = P(1 + i)^t`

The formulas may look similar, but their growth assumptions differ.

## Checkpoint
If previously earned interest itself earns interest in the next period, use a compound model—not a simple-interest model.
