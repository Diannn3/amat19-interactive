A proposition is either **true (T)** or **false (F)**. With two simple propositions, there are four assignments; with three, there are eight. The Truth Table lab follows the course handout's deterministic assignment order and connective semantics.

The lab treats `∨` as **inclusive OR** and `→` as **material implication**. Select a result cell to inspect the operand values and the rule that produced it.
