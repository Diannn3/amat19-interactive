## Supplemental status
Expected value and variance appear in the older probability material but are not explicit in the current course outline.

For a discrete random variable, `E[X] = Σ xP(X=x)`. Variance can be calculated as `E[X²] - E[X]²`. The lab keeps probabilities as exact rational numbers so rounding does not change whether they sum to 1.
