## Current-guide learning outcomes represented

The product supports the course-level goals to:
1. explain concepts using synchronized formulas/tables/graphs/timelines;
2. solve conceptual finite-math problems with deterministic tools;
3. formulate models through proposition structures, probability universes, cash-flow timelines, matrices, LP constraints and payoff matrices.

## Public-content boundary

Learner-facing examples, lessons and mixed practice are original or generated. The app does not reproduce full historical exam questions, answer keys or slide pages. This boundary is checked by `scripts/audit-content.mjs` and remains a human-review requirement as content grows.
